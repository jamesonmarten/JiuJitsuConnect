# MMA Connect Business Strategy & Go-to-Market Plan
*by Dev Cabin Technologies*

## 🎯 Executive Summary

MMA Connect is positioned as the premier B2B2C platform for martial arts gyms, providing lead generation, member management, and community building tools. Our freemium model attracts gyms with free licenses while generating revenue through premium features and scaled licensing.

---

## 💰 Revenue Model & Pricing Structure

### Licensing Tiers

#### **Free License** (Gym Acquisition Tool)
- **Cost**: $0/month
- **Members**: Up to 10 active members
- **Features**: 
  - Basic gym listing in directory
  - Up to 5 leads per month
  - Basic member profiles
  - Community access for members
- **Purpose**: Hook gyms with immediate value

#### **Basic License** 
- **Cost**: $29/month or $290/year (20% discount)
- **Members**: Up to 50 active members  
- **Features**:
  - Enhanced gym profile with photos/videos
  - Unlimited leads + lead tracking dashboard
  - Member progress tracking tools
  - Priority placement in gym finder
  - Email support

#### **Premium License**
- **Cost**: $79/month or $790/year (17% discount)
- **Members**: Up to 200 active members
- **Features**: 
  - All Basic features
  - Advanced analytics & reporting
  - Automated lead nurturing campaigns
  - Custom gym branding options
  - Instructor performance tracking
  - Phone support

#### **Enterprise License**
- **Cost**: $199/month or $1,990/year (17% discount)
- **Members**: Unlimited
- **Features**:
  - All Premium features
  - Multi-location management
  - API access for custom integrations
  - Dedicated account manager
  - White-label options
  - Custom development support

## 🎯 Target Market Segments

### Primary: Mid-sized MMA/BJJ Gyms (20-100 members)
- **Pain Points**: Lead generation, member retention, competition tracking
- **Budget**: $50-150/month for software solutions
- **Decision Makers**: Gym owners, head instructors
- **Sales Cycle**: 2-4 weeks

### Secondary: Large Multi-discipline Facilities (100+ members)
- **Pain Points**: Managing multiple programs, instructor coordination
- **Budget**: $200-500/month for comprehensive solutions
- **Decision Makers**: Business managers, franchise owners
- **Sales Cycle**: 1-3 months

### Tertiary: Independent Instructors & Small Studios
- **Pain Points**: Finding students, building credibility
- **Budget**: $20-50/month
- **Decision Makers**: Individual instructors
- **Sales Cycle**: 1-2 weeks
