## Platform-Specific Best Practices

### Instagram:
- Use 3-5 hashtags in caption, rest in first comment
- Post when your audience is most active (typically evenings)
- Use Stories for behind-the-scenes content
- Engage with martial arts influencers

### Facebook:
- Longer-form content performs better
- Ask questions to boost engagement
- Share in relevant martial arts groups
- Use Facebook Events for community meetups

### Twitter/X:
- Tweet 3-5 times per day
- Engage in martial arts Twitter conversations
- Use trending hashtags when relevant
- Retweet user success stories

### TikTok:
- Post consistently (daily if possible)
- Use trending sounds with martial arts themes
- Collaborate with martial arts creators
- Keep videos under 30 seconds for maximum engagement

### LinkedIn:
- Focus on business/professional angle
- Share entrepreneurship journey
- Connect with gym owners and martial arts business professionals
- Post 2-3 times per week maximum
